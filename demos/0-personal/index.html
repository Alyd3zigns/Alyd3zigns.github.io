My Life

Little sister

My mom and I have always had a weird relationship. I learned most of what I know, from her, from how to cook to how to live in the moment. As a kid I didn't think about the future almost at all. I just knew I was a kid and those bigger people were adults.

My mom has always been hard working and slaking off was her biggest reward. She loves holidays, especially the ones that celebrate her in some way or the ones that let her stay home. /
